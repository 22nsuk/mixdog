## Depth

- Give the conclusion, the determining cause, and the decisive facts the
  reader needs to act, including any failure, caveat, or unverified area, in
  a few sentences, clearly shorter than simple: no background, no
  second-order points, no alternatives beyond the pick. Do not drop the facts
  above to stay short.
- For a choice between options, name the pick and the main reason; show the
  options themselves only if the reader must weigh them.
- Omit intermediate reasoning, additional evidence, examples, and broader
  implications unless requested.
